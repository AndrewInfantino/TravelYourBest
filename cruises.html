    Why we cruise

    Wake up somewhere new, every single day.

    We love cruising. For us, it's the most exceptional kind of vacation: it's all-inclusive, the shows are great, the service is great, the food is great, and every day you wake up somewhere new. Looking out onto the sea never gets old, and travelling to new places is so much fun.

    Our tip: sail from Miami or Fort Lauderdale. Every one of our cruises has left from one of these two ports. We've found that the best cruise prices are from here, and South Florida is the easiest place for us to reach, in between our lives in Canada, the Dominican Republic and Spain. Miami is also a fantastic city to spend a day or two in before you board. Read our Miami & Fort Lauderdale guide →

    Cruise 1 Our favourite

    Royal Caribbean

    Oasis of the Seas was the largest ship we've ever been on, and it was incredible. It feels like a floating city, with whole neighbourhoods on board: a park full of real trees, a seaside boardwalk with a carousel, a promenade of shops and bars, and shows every night.

    ShipOasis of the Seas
    When4 to 10 January 2026
    Length6 nights
    FromMiami / Fort Lauderdale
    A floating city01

    Boarding Oasis of the Seas

    Seeing Oasis of the Seas at the pier for the first time is something else: it towers over everything around it. Boarding is quick and organised, and from the moment you step on, there's something to do every minute of the day.

    Our favourite spot02

    The Boardwalk at night

    The Boardwalk is like a seaside promenade in the middle of the ship, open to the sky between the cabins, with a carousel, restaurants and the AquaTheater at the end. At night it lights up in every colour and it's pure magic.

    Neighbourhoods03

    Central Park & the Royal Promenade

    Yes, there's a real park on board: Central Park is full of trees and plants, with restaurants all around. Below it, the Royal Promenade is a long indoor street of cafés, bars and shops, complete with a vintage car. We loved dressing up for dinner and strolling here in the evening.

    Adrenaline04

    The zip line & nights out

    Andrew flew across the ship on the zip line, nine decks above the Boardwalk. In the evenings, the ship comes alive with Broadway-style shows, live music, bars and dancing, so there's never a dull moment.

    Cruise 2

    MSC Cruises

    Our MSC cruise was an exceptional 7-day trip where we had an amazing time. MSC ships are elegant and stylish, with glittering crystal staircases, and the itinerary took us to Mexico and to MSC's own private island in the Bahamas.

    Elegant05

    Life on board MSC

    MSC has a very European, elegant feel. The famous crystal staircases sparkle in the middle of the ship, and we loved dressing up for dinner and the evening shows.

    Private island06

    Ocean Cay

    Ocean Cay MSC Marine Reserve is MSC's private island in the Bahamas: soft white sand, turquoise water and a red-and-white lighthouse. It was one of the most beautiful beach days we've had on a cruise.

    Cruise 3

    Norwegian Cruise Line

    Our sailing on the Norwegian Gem was a short, fun getaway: three nights of sun, sea and relaxing. You can't miss the ship, with its colourful painted hull.

    ShipNorwegian Gem
    When27 to 30 June 2025
    Length3 nights
    FromMiami / Fort Lauderdale
    Colourful07

    The Norwegian Gem

    The Norwegian Gem is smaller than the mega-ships, which makes it easy to get around and very relaxed. NCL's "freestyle" cruising means no fixed dinner times, so you can do things at your own pace.

    Private island08

    Great Stirrup Cay

    Great Stirrup Cay is NCL's private island in the Bahamas, with beach swings, palm trees and clear water. Perfect for a lazy beach day.

    Cruise 4

    Celebrity Cruises

    Our first taste of Celebrity was a 3-night Nassau getaway: a short, easy escape to the Bahamas. Celebrity has a modern, upscale feel, with stylish design everywhere you look, great food, excellent service and some of the best nights out we've had at sea.

    ShipCelebrity
    WhenAugust 2024
    Length3 nights
    FromMiami / Fort Lauderdale

    See our Nassau guide →

    Modern & elegant09

    All aboard Celebrity

    You can't miss the giant X on the side of a Celebrity ship. On board, everything feels sleek and design-led, from the lounges to the cabins, and it's a little calmer and more grown-up than the mega-ships.

    Sun & sea10

    The pool deck & ocean views

    Days at sea are all about the pool deck: a swim, a hot tub with a view and a drink in the sun. And nothing beats standing at the railing looking out over the open ocean.

    Great food11

    Dinner in the main dining room

    The main dining room is stunning, with sparkling chandeliers and floor-to-ceiling windows. We loved dressing up for dinner every night, and the food and service were excellent.

    Nights out12

    Deck parties & shows

    The nights were unforgettable: a glow party under the stars on the top deck, with everyone dancing with light-up X sticks, and great shows in the theatre.

    Thailand

    Bangkok & Phuket

    two cities · 9 must-see spots

    Jump to Bangkok Phuket

    The trip

    Spectacular. A real dream.

    Thailand gave us two completely different trips in one. We started in Bangkok — temples, glass skydecks, neon Chinatown and markets that spill onto train tracks — then flew south to Phuket for elephants, island-hopping and slow beach sunsets. Here's exactly where every shot was taken in each city, and the tours we booked to make it easy. We visited in March 2025.

    City one

    Bangkok

    Bangkok · Most iconic01

    The Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew

    📍 Grand Palace · Old City (Rattanakosin)

    An overwhelming, glittering complex of gold spires and mirrored temples, home to the Emerald Buddha. It's Bangkok's must-see — we'd just say get there early before the heat and the crowds peak. One thing to know: the dress code is strict (shoulders and knees covered for everyone); we brought layers, and you can rent a cover-up at the gate if needed.

    Cover shoulders & kneesGo early
    Bangkok · Best view02

    The Mahanakhon SkyWalk

    📍 King Power Mahanakhon · Silom / Sathon

    Thailand's highest observation deck, with a glass floor that shows the whole city dropping away beneath your feet — equal parts thrilling and terrifying. We went in the late afternoon so we could watch the light change over the skyline (there's a clip of that view further down). Not for the faint-hearted, but the photos are unreal.

    Glass skydeckGo for sunset
    Bangkok · Best free view03

    The Golden Mount (Wat Saket)

    📍 Wat Saket · Old City

    A golden stupa on top of an artificial hill, reached by a gentle spiral of about 300 steps lined with bells and flags. It was one of our favourite Bangkok surprises — the terrace at the top gives a full 360° view over the old city for barely a couple of dollars. We went late afternoon as it cooled down.

    ~300 steps360° city view
    Bangkok · After dark04

    Chinatown & a night at the fights

    📍 Yaowarat Road → Rajadamnern Stadium

    Yaowarat is a wall of neon and, we think, the best street food in the city — we came hungry and left very full. Another Bangkok night we'll never forget was ringside at a Muay Thai match: the energy in the stadium is electric, and it's a real slice of Thai culture, not a tourist show.

    Bangkok · Best day trip05

    The floating & train markets

    📍 Damnoen Saduak & Maeklong Railway Market · outside Bangkok

    Two of the most surreal mornings of the trip, and easy to combine. At the floating market we drifted the canals in a long-tail past vendors selling everything from noodles to fruit; at the railway market, stalls literally slide back off the tracks as a real train squeezes through inches away, then reopen like nothing happened. We booked the two together as one day trip — there are clips of both below.

    City two

    Phuket

    Phuket · Most memorable01

    A morning with rescued elephants

    📍 Elephant sanctuary · Phuket

    Hands down one of the most special things we've ever done. We spent a half-day feeding, walking and bathing rescued elephants down by the water — no riding, just time with these gentle giants. If you do one thing in Phuket, make it this, and choose an ethical, no-riding sanctuary like the one we went with.

    Phuket · Best adventure02

    James Bond Island & Phang Nga Bay

    📍 Phang Nga Bay · day trip from Phuket

    Towering limestone karsts jutting straight out of emerald water — the whole of Phang Nga Bay looks unreal. We stood in front of the famous James Bond Island (Ko Tapu), sea-kayaked into hidden caves and lagoons, and had lunch at Koh Panyee, a fishing village built entirely on stilts over the water. A full, brilliant day on the sea.

    Phuket · Best panorama03

    The Big Buddha & the viewpoints

    📍 Big Buddha & the island's clifftop viewpoints · Phuket

    The 45-metre Big Buddha sits on a hilltop with rows of brass wishing bells and a sweeping view over the south of the island. We paired it with Phuket's famous viewpoints looking down over the turquoise bays — best strung together on a half-day city tour that also drops you at the night market for dinner.

    Phuket · Most relaxing04

    Phuket's beaches & sunsets

    📍 Phuket's west-coast beaches

    After all the temples and markets, we slowed right down on Phuket's west-coast beaches. Warm water, soft sand, sunset cocktails and skies that turned the whole horizon gold — the perfect way to end a trip. We'd just pick a beach on the west side and time it for golden hour.

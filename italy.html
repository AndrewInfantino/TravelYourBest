    Italy

    The country we keep coming back to.

    Italy is the country we've explored the most, and every region feels like a different trip: the ancient wonders of Rome, the Duomo in Milan, the cliffs of the Amalfi Coast, the villas of Lake Como, Renaissance Florence, the canals of Venice and the street food of Sicily. Here are all of our Italy guides in one place.

    Our tip: Italy is easy to combine. The fast trains link Milan, Florence, Rome, Venice and Naples in just a few hours, so you can see two or three regions in a single trip.

    The trip

    Fashion, design & a marble masterpiece.

    Milan is stylish, fast-paced and easy to love. The Duomo alone is worth the trip, and walking on its rooftop among the spires was a highlight for us. Add the glass-domed Galleria, the castle and some of the best gelato in Italy, and you have a perfect city break. Milan is also a great base for a day trip to Lake Como.

    Our pro tips: book the Duomo rooftop and Leonardo's Last Supper ahead of time, because they sell out. The metro is quick and simple for getting around, and the historic centre is very walkable.

    The city

    Milan's must-sees

    Everything here is within a short walk of Piazza del Duomo.

    Must-see01

    The Duomo di Milano

    📍 Piazza del Duomo

    The Duomo is breathtaking: a gigantic Gothic cathedral in white and pink marble, covered in spires and more than 3,000 statues. It took nearly six centuries to finish. Come back at golden hour, when the whole facade glows.

    Golden hourCover shoulders inside
    Our favourite02

    The Duomo rooftop terraces

    📍 On top of the cathedral

    Don't miss going up to the rooftop. You walk among the marble spires, flying buttresses and statues, with views over the whole city. It was one of our favourite experiences in Milan. You can take the lift or the stairs: the stairs ticket is cheaper.

    Don't miss itLift or stairs
    Iconic03

    Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

    📍 Next to Piazza del Duomo

    Right next to the Duomo, the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II is one of the oldest shopping arcades in the world, with a stunning glass dome and mosaic floors. Grab a gelato and wander. Tradition says you should spin on your heel on the mosaic bull for good luck.

    History04

    Sforzesco Castle

    📍 Piazza Castello

    The red-brick Sforzesco Castle was home to the dukes of Milan. Walk around its moats and courtyards, then continue into Parco Sempione behind it, a big green park that's perfect for a break from the city.

    Unexpected05

    Piazza Affari

    📍 The financial district

    In front of the Milan stock exchange stands one of the city's most talked-about artworks: Maurizio Cattelan's L.O.V.E. sculpture, a giant marble hand. It's a fun, quirky stop between the Duomo and the castle, and you might even spot a few supercars parked nearby.
